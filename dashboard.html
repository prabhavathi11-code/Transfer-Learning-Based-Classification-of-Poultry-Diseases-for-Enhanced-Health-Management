Comprehensive Avian Pathology Guide

Clinical characteristics, pathogen profiles, and prevention strategies for the 4 conditions identified by PoultryDetect.

Coccidiosis
Protozoan
  • Etiology: Eimeria tenella and related coccidian protozoa infecting intestinal lining.
  • Transmission: Ingestion of sporulated oocysts from damp litter, contaminated water, or feeders.
  • Clinical Signs: Bloody or dark mucoid droppings, ruffled plumage, lethargy, decreased feed conversion.
  • Control & Therapy: Administer Amprolium or Toltrazuril; maintain dry litter and regular bedding turning.
Newcastle Disease (ND)
Viral Contagion
  • Etiology: Avian Paramyxovirus Serotype 1 (APMV-1), highly virulent viral agent.
  • Transmission: Airborne aerosol inhalation, bodily secretions, infected feces, wild birds.
  • Clinical Signs: Torticolis (twisted neck), respiratory clicking, greenish watery diarrhea, paralysis.
  • Control & Therapy: No direct antiviral cure; strict quarantine, supportive electrolytes, routine LaSota vaccination.
Salmonellosis / Pullorum
Bacterial
  • Etiology: Salmonella enterica (serovars Pullorum / Gallinarum).
  • Transmission: Transovarian (vertical from hen to chick) or horizontal via rodent droppings and contaminated feed.
  • Clinical Signs: Chalky white paste adhering to vent, somnolence, huddling near heat sources, septicemia.
  • Control & Therapy: Enrofloxacin or Amoxicillin in drinking water; strict rodent control and chlorinated water.
Healthy Flock Standards
Optimal
  • Physiological Markers: Bright red, firm comb and wattles; clear, round eyes; smooth clean plumage.
  • Behavior: Alert, active foraging, continuous vocalization, immediate feed response.
  • Fecal Consistency: Firm brownish-grey droppings with distinct white uric acid cap.
  • Management: Clean water ad libitum, 18-20% crude protein starter/grower mash, adequate ventilation.

🦠 Understanding Coccidiosis in Poultry

Coccidiosis is one of the most economically devastating diseases affecting modern poultry farms worldwide. Early recognition through visible fecal changes and automated image screening prevents rapid flock mortality.

💉 Newcastle Disease: Prevention First

This viral infection spreads at lightning speed through pens. Strict biosecurity footbaths, bird segregation, and timely vaccination schedules form the only dependable line of defense against catastrophic outbreak.

By PoultryDetect Biosecurity Team

🐔 How to Maintain a Healthy Flock

Regular health checks, clean cool water, balanced nutritional protein ratios, and continuous ventilation play crucial roles in disease prevention. Utilizing AI diagnostic tools enables early detection before widespread transmission.

Deep Learning Model Architecture

Transfer learning pipeline using MobileNetV2 fine-tuned on categorized avian images.

PoultryDetect uses a fine-tuned MobileNetV2 convolutional neural network trained on over 7,000 labeled images from Roboflow. MobileNetV2 uses depthwise separable convolutions and inverted residual bottleneck blocks, providing high diagnostic accuracy with lightweight parameter requirements suitable for low-latency web and edge inference.
