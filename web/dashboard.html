Poultry Pathology & Disease Reference

Comprehensive microbiological, clinical, and preventive guide for avian pathologies identified by PoultryVision.

Coccidiosis

Protozoan (Eimeria spp.)
Critical
  • Transmission: Ingestion of sporulated oocysts from contaminated litter, feed, or drinking water.
  • Key Symptoms: Bloody diarrhea, ruffled feathers, severe weight loss, lethargy, reduced feed consumption.
  • Incubation: 4 to 7 days post-ingestion.
  • Medication: Amprolium, Toltrazuril, Sulfaquinoxaline in water supply.
  • Prevention: Maintain bone-dry bedding, avoid high bird density, rotational anticoccidial feed programs.

Newcastle Disease (ND)

Avian Paramyxovirus Serotype 1 (APMV-1)
High Contagion
  • Transmission: Aerosol droplet inhalation, bodily secretions, infected feces, wild birds.
  • Key Symptoms: Torticolis (twisted neck), gasping, coughing, greenish watery diarrhea, paralysis of wings and legs.
  • Incubation: 2 to 15 days (average 5 days).
  • Treatment: No direct viral cure; secondary antibiotic treatment with electrolyte supportive therapy.
  • Prevention: Routine vaccination schedule (LaSota / B1 strains), strict quarantine and farm gate sanitation.

Salmonellosis / Pullorum

Salmonella enterica (Pullorum / Gallinarum)
Bacterial
  • Transmission: Vertical transmission via breeder eggs, or horizontal through infected feed, rodents, and water.
  • Key Symptoms: Chalky white diarrhea pasted around vent, somnolence, huddling near heaters, breathing difficulty.
  • Incubation: 1 to 3 days in young chicks.
  • Medication: Enrofloxacin, Amoxicillin, or Neomycin under veterinary supervision.
  • Prevention: Rodent pest extermination, pelletized heated feed, water chlorination, testing of parent flocks.

Healthy Flock Standards

Optimal Physiological Baseline
Normal
  • Appearance: Bright red, erect comb and wattles; clear, round eyes; smooth clean feathers.
  • Behavior: Alert, active foraging, continuous vocalization, immediate response to feed distribution.
  • Droppings: Firm, brownish-grey with distinct white uric acid caps.
  • Vital Metrics: Normal body temperature (41-42°C), consistent daily egg lay rate, steady weekly weight growth.
  • Maintenance: Clean cool water ad libitum, balanced 18-20% crude protein starter/grower mash, adequate ventilation.
